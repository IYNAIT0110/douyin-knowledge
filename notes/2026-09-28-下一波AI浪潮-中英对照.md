# Part A · 核心论点（逐句对照）
# Part A · Core Arguments (Sentence by Sentence)

## A1. 能力是玩出来的 / Capabilities Are Discovered, Not Specified

**EN** Even for OpenAI, it's like we train models and then we discover their capabilities.
> 即便是 OpenAI，也是先把模型训练出来，然后才**发现**它的能力。

**EN** Like we don't, benchmarks don't tell you everything.
> 就是说，benchmark 并不会告诉你全部。

**EN** We have to play quite a bit with the models themselves to sort of realize, like, oh, you know, maybe we haven't thought about benefiting from it in like this specific way.
> 我们得大量地把玩这些模型本身，才会慢慢意识到：哦，原来我们还没想过可以这样用它。

## A2. 笔记本本身成了约束 / The Laptop Becomes the Constraint

**EN** Another thing that, you know, you realize when you have very, very powerful models is that your laptop kind of becomes a constraint in and by itself.
> 还有一件事，当你有了非常非常强的模型时就会意识到：你的笔记本本身就成了一种约束。

**EN** The amount of work that you can do on a laptop, it was designed for humans, right?
> 你在一台笔记本上能干多少活 —— 它本来就是**为人类设计**的，对吧？

**EN** All these things are human constraints. The model doesn't have the same constraints.
> 这些都是人类约束。模型并不受同样的约束。

## A3. 瓶颈换地方了 / The Bottleneck Moved

**EN** The bandwidth constraint changes.
> 带宽约束变了。

**EN** The CPU now becomes the bandwidth, like literally tool calls.
> CPU 现在变成了带宽 —— 真的，就是工具调用。

**EN** Network, tool call, any kind of overhead in the stack becomes the limiting factor.
> 网络、工具调用，栈里任何形式的开销都成了限制因素。

## A4. 递归自我改进的真正形态 / What Recursive Self-Improvement Actually Looks Like

**EN** Recursive self-improvement is obviously a huge topic right now and it's most often, I think, applied to research and models developing other models.
> 递归自我改进显然眼下是个大话题，而它最常被用在研究上，也就是"模型造模型"。

**EN** But what we are seeing a ton of success with is using those models to develop the infrastructure that is on the critical path of using those models, which is also a form of recursive self-improvement.
> 但我们看到大量成功的是另一件事：用这些模型去开发**位于"使用这些模型的关键路径上"的基础设施** —— 这也是一种递归自我改进。

**EN** So it's all one big system.
> 所以这就是一个大系统。

**EN** And so of course we're doing that. Like if we were not doing that I think that would be pretty silly.
> 所以我们当然在做。要是我们不做，那才叫蠢。

## A5. 为什么 Google 没能发出来 / Why Google Couldn't Ship It

**EN** DeepMind was not set up to ship product.
> DeepMind 的架构就不是为了发产品而设的。

**EN** OpenAI has a very bottoms up culture. Like it's a very empowering culture.
> OpenAI 是一种非常自下而上的文化。一种非常赋权的文化。

**EN** There's very little stop energy in general for like new product ideas, which is exhilarating and fun.
> 总的来说，对新点子几乎没有"叫停的能量"，这让人兴奋，也很好玩。

**EN** Being willing to disrupt yourself — being able to identify when is the right moment to go and invest in them, even though it means like maybe reallocating resources from, you know, the main gig.
> 愿意颠覆自己 —— 能识别出什么时候该去投入新东西，哪怕这意味着可能要从主业那边抽调资源。

**EN** But it's very hard, but it's super important to be able to do that.
> 这非常难，但能做到这件事极其重要。

## A6. 标签只是人类发明的抽象 / Labels Are Just Abstractions Humans Invented

**EN** We come up with labels of like a software engineer, a designer. Like, you know, these are just human concepts that we have invented to deal with abstractions because the reality is too complex for us to handle.
> 我们发明出"软件工程师""设计师"这样的标签。这些只是**人类为了应付抽象而发明的概念**，因为现实太复杂，我们处理不了。

**EN** But individuals are individual, they have their own, they're somewhere on the spectrum.
> 但每个人就是每个人，他们有自己的位置，他们处在光谱上的某一点。

**EN** Every time you sort of like lean into something that is more natural, like humans just choose the path of least resistance.
> 每当你朝更自然的方向靠拢，人类就是会选阻力最小的那条路。

## A7. 给担心 AI 的人 / For People Nervous About AI

**EN** Access to incredible intelligence will become ubiquitous, and it's only possible when you push the efficiency, you know, like month after month after month, year after year.
> 获得极强智能这件事会变得无处不在，而这只有在你月复一月、年复一年地推效率时才可能。

**EN** I feel like I get a lot of support that I otherwise, you know, it would be hard for me to get, and it allows me, for example, to be more informed when I go see my doctor.
> 我觉得我得到了很多原本很难获得的支持；比如说，它让我去看医生的时候是更有准备的。

**EN** Talking to others and then getting inspired by how others use it and benefit from it is like a great way to, you know, maybe start considering how you could benefit from it.
> 跟别人聊聊，被别人"怎么用、怎么从中受益"所启发 —— 这是开始思考"我能怎么受益"的好办法。

# Part B · The Argument Unfolds (Full Paragraph)

**EN** So I think about two different categories of problems. Like the first one is building the very best personal AGI or the personal agent that will be in the flow with you, proactive, raise important new ideas when it can find some, be very, very efficient at doing exactly what you want. It doesn't matter whether it's a technical problem or, you know, it's just more like research or advice, like you can do it all. And it's like super, super tailored to you. This is a very important thing. And it's like deeply rooted in the understanding of you as a human, you as an individual that is unique. That's one category of problem. The other category of problems is like full on automation, you know, where you're more building intelligent systems that can take care of a very complex process. For example, you know, going and looking at production logs and automatically doing performance optimizations, or looking at regressions and automatically patching them. Without a human in the loop, or like, you know, very, very minimal where you only need to approve a high risk action.
> 我会把它想成两类不同的问题。第一类，是造出最好的"个人 AGI"或者说个人 agent —— 它跟你在同一个心流里，能主动，能在发现苗头时提出重要的新想法，并且非常非常高效地做你真正想做的事。它是技术问题还是别的都不重要，可能更像是研究，或者咨询 —— 它都能干。而且它是极度、极度为你定制的。这件事非常重要，它深深植根于对你的理解：把你当作一个人，当作一个独一无二的人。这是第一类问题。另一类问题是全面自动化 —— 你在造的是那种能接管一个非常复杂流程的智能系统。比如说，去翻生产日志，自动做性能优化；或者发现 regression 就自动把补丁打上。人在环外，或者参与度极低 —— 只需要你审批高风险动作。

# 文末速览清单（中文）

- 能力是**玩**出来的：benchmark 只给下限，真实用法增量来自拿自己的任务去撞。
- **笔记本是为人类设计的**，模型不受同样的约束 → 下一代模型需要的资源多于一笔记本。
- 速度提上去后，瓶颈从生成转移到 **tool calls / 网络 / CPU**；工具调用密集场景只有 3-4 倍，不是 14 倍。
- **递归自我改进**最实在的形态：用模型去优化**跑模型的关键路径**（kernel、serving、调度），然后把收益分享出去。
- 组织能力 = **愿意颠覆自己**：难在从主业抽调资源，不在判断。
- 快速 ship 要用"简洁感和质量自豪感"来**平衡**，否则就成一锅粥。
- **标签（工程师／设计师）只是人类为应付抽象发明的概念** —— 界面该按个体自适应，不按角色分叉。
- 采用率的预测器不是"功能多强"，而是**阻力有多小**。
- 对担心 AI 的人：他不讲大道理，讲效率复利 + 已经发生的私人用途 + "让你见医生时更有准备"。
