## A3. 在线 RL 不是你以为的那种自我改进 / Live RL Is Not the Self-Improvement You Pictured

**EN** Here is the distinction that most coverage misses.
> **中** 这里有一个大多数报道都漏掉的区别。

**EN** Live RL is much closer to what the field calls test-time training than to "the model reinvents itself".
> **中** 在线强化学习更接近业内说的"测试时训练"，而不是"模型重新发明自己"。

**EN** The model is not being asked to synthesise its own environment and its own training data on the training side.
> **中** 它并不是被要求回到训练侧、自己合成环境和自己造训练数据。

**EN** Instead it takes what real users actually do in production and feeds that straight back in as reinforcement signal.
> **中** 而是把生产环境里真实用户的实际使用，直接拿回来当作强化学习的信号。

**EN** The pipeline underneath is fairly conventional; the point is who it no longer needs.
> **中** 底下的训练流程其实相当传统；关键在它不再需要谁。

**EN** The stated aim is to shrink the dependence on experts — above all, the rare people who know how to train models.
> **中** 讲出来的目标就是缩小对专家的依赖 —— 尤其是那些懂得怎么训练模型的稀缺人才。

**EN** That is why this route fits Google: they are bleeding talent but sitting on the deepest pile of compute.
> **中** 这正是这条路线适合谷歌的原因：他们在流失人才，却坐在最厚的一堆算力上。

**EN** If the path works, every later model is just a matter of adding resources.
> **中** 只要路径可行，后面无论 Flash 还是 Pro，都只是加资源的事。

## A4. 智谱：连流水线也要自己造 / Zhipu: Rebuild the Whole Pipeline

**EN** Zhipu went to the Hong Kong exchange and raised roughly HK$39.3 billion — somewhere north of five billion US dollars.
> **中** 智谱在港交所配售新股，募资约 393 亿港元 —— 折合五十多亿美元。

**EN** About sixty percent of it points at one word: RSI.
> **中** 其中约六成指向同一个词：RSI。

**EN** Their name for it is fully self-training: the next generation trains inside the environment the previous generation built.
> **中** 他们给它起名叫"完全自训练"：下一代模型，在上一代模型搭建的环境里训练。

**EN** Three things have to become self-sufficient: the data, the environment, and the infrastructure.
> **中** 有三样东西必须自给自足：数据、环境、基础设施。

**EN** Data first: stop depending on human labels, and stop leaning on a stronger external model either.
> **中** 先说数据：摆脱人类标注，同时也别再依赖更强的外部模型。

**EN** The machinery is familiar — self-play, rule checks, execution checks, LLM as judge, and human spot-audits.
> **中** 手段是熟悉的那一套：自我对弈、规则校验、执行验证、LLM as a judge，加人工抽审。

**EN** The trick is self-certification: filter plus judge, pulling stable answers out of unstable generations.
> **中** 诀窍在于"自证有"：用 filter 加 judge，从不稳定的生成里捞出稳定的结果。

**EN** And where one instance tops out, several collaborating instances reach a quality none of them hits alone.
> **中** 而单个实例到顶的地方，靠多实例协作能达到谁都单独达不到的整体质量。

**EN** Whatever survives gets generated, filtered, accumulated, and poured back into pre-training, mid-training and post-training.
> **中** 活下来的数据被自动沉淀、筛选、累积，再回流到预训练、中训练和后训练阶段。

**EN** Second, the environment: agents collect and convert real tasks, attempt them, write their own verifiers, and check convergence themselves.
> **中** 第二是环境：智能体去采集并转化真实任务、自己试做、自己生成验证器、自己检查任务是否收敛。

**EN** The goal is to make the task environment something you can mass-produce and reuse, not commission one task at a time.
> **中** 目标是让任务环境变成可规模化生产、可复用的训练资源，而不是一个个去定制。

**EN** Coding for agent is the sharpest version of this: the model invents its own difficulties, then solves them.
> **中** Coding for agent 是这种做法最锋利的形态：模型自己给自己创造困难，再自己解决。

**EN** Third, the strangest layer: let the model optimise the training and inference stack it actually runs on.
> **中** 第三是最奇怪的一层：让模型去优化"它自己赖以运行"的那套训练与推理系统。

**EN** It already writes code, so point it at the operators, kernels, schedulers, caches and compilers underneath it.
> **中** 它既然会写代码，那就让它去优化底下的算子、内核、调度、缓存和编译。

**EN** That is an end-to-end optimisation of training infrastructure, done by the thing that runs on it.
> **中** 这是一次端到端的训练基础设施优化，而做优化的是跑在上面的那个模型自己。

**EN** The loop this creates is delightful — the AI helps design the chip, and the chip fits the model better.
> **中** 这个循环很迷人：AI 参与设计芯片，而芯片反过来更贴合模型的结构。

**EN** OpenAI's silicon effort runs on similar logic, and parts of DeepSeek's recent designs reportedly came from model suggestions.
> **中** OpenAI 的芯片项目也是类似逻辑，而 DeepSeek 近期设计里的不少灵感，据称也来自大模型给出的建议。

**EN** The stated goal is unglamorous and honest: more effective compute depth without more inference cost.
> **中** 讲出来的目标朴素而诚实：在不增加推理成本的前提下，加深有效计算深度。

**EN** It buys longer chains: more planning, more self-checking, on roughly the same bill.
> **中** 它换来的是更长的链路：更多的推理规划、更多的自我校验，而账单大致不变。

**EN** Only about fifteen percent of the raise goes to business development, strategic investment and possible acquisitions.
> **中** 募来的钱里只有约 15% 用于业务拓展、战略投资和潜在并购。

**EN** The core of the money is still one thing: raise the intelligence of the whole system.
> **中** 资金的核心仍然只有一件事：抬高整个系统的智能。

## A5. 阶跃：先做一把尺子 / StepFun: Build the Ruler First

**EN** StepFun did something quieter: together with Peking University and HKUST, they published a benchmark called Φ-Bench.
> **中** 阶跃星辰做了件更安静的事：联合北大、港科大发布了基准 Φ-Bench。

**EN** The question it asks is nasty: can a model improve the infrastructure that runs it?
> **中** 它问的问题很刁：模型能不能优化那个让它得以运行的基础设施？

**EN** At first glance that sounds like Zhipu's third pillar. Look at the actual tasks and it is something else.
> **中** 乍一看这跟智谱的第三根柱子很像。但看实际题目会发现是另一回事。

**EN** This is closer to teaching the whole intelligent system to learn on the job.
> **中** 它更像是让整个智能系统在工作中自我学习。

**EN** The benchmark covers three kinds of task, and the split is the interesting part.
> **中** 基准覆盖三类任务，而分类方式本身就是有意思的地方。

**EN** KFC, kernel function completion: you are handed the interface and the semantics, and must write a correct, fast kernel inside a fixed set of files.
> **中** KFC，算子函数补全：给你接口和输入输出语义，要求在限定的文件内写出正确且高效的算子。

**EN** The task is well specified; what is being tested is whether the model can iterate through multiple attempts and improve its own score.
> **中** 任务是明确定义的；考的是模型能否通过多次调用与学习不断提升自己的分数。

**EN** LHI, long-horizon implementation: real feature work inside a real codebase, handed over the way humans get it — an issue, an error, a bug report.
> **中** LHI，长周期实现：真实代码库里的真实功能开发，需求给的方式跟人收到的完全一样 —— 一条 issue、一段报错、一张 bug 单。

**EN** The model has to read the structure, locate the modules, touch several files, and keep testing until it actually runs end to end.
> **中** 模型要自己理解代码结构、定位相关模块、修改多个文件，并持续测试调试直到端到端跑通。

**EN** That is the self-improvement of a coding agent in the wild: fix the repo, and turn the feedback loop into a core module.
> **中** 这就是一个编码智能体在实战中的自我提升：修复代码库里的问题，并把"反馈—修复"变成解决问题的核心模块。

**EN** E2EO, end-to-end optimisation: here you get the real workload, the target and the constraints — but nobody tells you which path to take.
> **中** E2EO，端到端优化：这里给你真实工作负载、优化目标和约束条件，但没有任何人告诉你走哪条路。

**EN** The model must find the bottleneck itself, plan the change, and iterate across modules and layers.
> **中** 模型必须自己分析系统瓶颈、制定优化方案，并跨模块、跨层级地做代码修改和迭代优化。

**EN** They mined over ten thousand candidate sources and kept eighty-five, each reviewed by a human domain expert.
> **中** 他们挖出一万多个候选任务来源，最后只留下 85 个，每一个都由领域专家人工复核过。

**EN** The results are a bloodbath, and that is the point.
> **中** 结果一地鸡毛，而这恰恰是重点。

**EN** Even Claude Opus 5 scores only 36.53; the weakest model scores 13.31.
> **中** 连 Claude Opus 5 也只有 36.53 分，最低的模型只有 13.31 分。

**EN** What is being scored is not coding, though: it is whether the model learns, accumulates, and improves while solving something hard.
> **中** 但考的并不是写代码，而是模型在解决复杂问题的过程中会不会学习、会不会积累、能不能提升最终结果。

**EN** Now the field has a ruler, so it finally knows how far it still has to go.
> **中** 现在这个领域有了尺子，终于知道自己还差多远。

## A6. 顺走的一份礼包 / An Unexpected Gift

**EN** The team also studied the full optimisation traces and named three traits of a model that does Infra work well.
> **中** 团队还研究了完整的优化轨迹，总结出擅长 Infra 工程的模型具备的三个特质。

**EN** Plan before acting: filter hypotheses cheaply instead of rewriting code on instinct.
> **中** 谋定而后动：先用低成本筛选假设，别凭直觉改代码。

**EN** Learn from experiments: design them so each attempt produces information, controlling variables and noise.
> **中** 实验求新知：设计好实验让每一次尝试都产出信息，控制变量与噪声。

**EN** Be slow to conclude: never read a performance change as a cause before ruling out confounds.
> **中** 谨慎下结论：在排除混杂因素之前，绝不把性能变化读成因果。

**EN** Those are not machine traits; they are the standard discipline of any good engineer.
> **中** 这几条不是机器特有，而是任何一个好工程师的基本功。

## A7. 第三条路升的是 skill，不是权重 / The Third Route Upgrades Skills, Not Weights

**EN** StepFun's route leads somewhere most people do not expect.
> **中** 阶跃这条路通向一个多数人没想到的地方。

**EN** The point is not to make the model depend less on human players, but to let it explore the system on its own.
> **中** 重点不是让模型少依赖"人类玩家"，而是让模型自己去探索这个系统。

**EN** Experience accumulates during that exploration, and accumulated experience can be written down as skills.
> **中** 探索过程中会积累经验，而积累下来的经验可以写成 skill。

**EN** Those skills live in the harness, not in the weights — so this is an upgrade at the harness layer, not an upgrade of the model.
> **中** 这些 skill 写在 harness 里，不在权重里 —— 所以这是 harness 层面的升级，而不是模型层面的升级。

**EN** And the skills are not wasted: the traces they leave behind become training data for the next model.
> **中** 而且这些 skill 不会浪费：它们留下的轨迹可以当作训练数据去训下一代模型。

**EN** None of the three routes is wrong, and they are not mutually exclusive.
> **中** 三条路线没有谁错，彼此也不冲突。

**EN** While the definition of RSI stays unsettled, every company is free to publish its own version of it.
> **中** 在 RSI 的定义还没定下来之前，每家公司都可以提供自己的 RSI 设想。

**EN** The target underneath all three is the same: depend less on experts and outside resources, and improve yourself.
> **中** 三条路线底下那个目标是一样的：减少对专家与外部资源的依赖，实现自我进化。

# Part B · 论证展开（整段对照）
# Part B · The Argument in Detail (Paragraph by Paragraph)

## B1. Why Everyone Defines RSI Differently

**EN**
There is a pattern hiding in this episode, and it is worth naming because it will repeat every year. Nobody in this industry defines their goal honestly in the abstract; they define it in the direction their own balance sheet can support. Google is short of elite researchers and long on compute, so RSI becomes online reinforcement learning — a route where the model learns from live traffic and the improvement comes from scale rather than from a handful of irreplaceable people. Zhipu is short of autonomous data and environments and long on cash, so RSI becomes fully self-training — rebuild the pipeline so that data, tasks and even the training infrastructure are produced by the system itself. StepFun, which cannot yet win the model race outright, does the third thing available to any outsider: it writes the exam. Look at the three announcements side by side and they stop looking like three answers to one question; they look like three questions each company can already answer.

> **中**
> 这一段里藏着一个模式，值得单独命名，因为它每年都会重演。这个行业里没有人是在抽象意义上诚实地定义目标的；大家都是朝着自家资产负债表能支撑的方向去定义。谷歌缺的是顶尖研究员，有的是算力，于是 RSI 变成"在线强化学习" —— 一条靠线上流量驱动、靠算力规模而不是靠几个不可替代的人来推动改进的路线。智谱缺的是自主的数据与环境，有的是现金，于是 RSI 变成"完全自训练" —— 把流水线整个重做，让数据、任务乃至训练基础设施都由系统自己生成。阶跃星辰暂时赢不了模型竞赛，于是走了任何局外人都能走的第三条路：**自己出题**。把三份公告并排看，它们就不再像"对同一个问题的三种答案"，而更像三家公司各自已经会答的那道题。

## B2. Google's Wager, Read Charitably

**EN**
Read Google's summer charitably and it makes sense. Six weeks, three Flash models, and no flagship launch looks like weakness until you notice what each release actually tests. Online reinforcement learning is worthless without live traffic, and every Flash release is another cohort of users producing real feedback for free. The bet is that if this loop works, intelligence stops being gated by how many world-class researchers you can retain, and becomes gated by how much compute you can point at it — which flips Google's weakness into its strength. Read it uncharitably and it is a company shipping point releases while its rivals ship frontier models. Both readings fit the same facts, which is the useful thing about having them both in your head. The test is simple enough: whichever flagship comes after these Flash releases will tell you whether online RL was the plan or the excuse.

> **中**
> 善意地解读谷歌这个夏天，是说得通的。六周三个 Flash 模型、旗舰产品缺席，看起来像乏力，直到你注意到每一次发布到底在测什么。在线强化学习没有真实流量就没有价值，而每发布一个 Flash，就等于多一批用户在免费制造真实反馈。赌的是：如果这个循环跑通，智能的上限就不再取决于你能留住多少世界级研究员，而取决于你能投入多少算力 —— 这就把谷歌的弱点翻转成了优势。恶意地解读，这就是一家公司一边发布小版本迭代、一边看着对手发布前沿模型。两种解读都能对上同一批事实，而这正是两种都要留在脑子里的价值所在。检验方法很简单：这几个 Flash 之后的那款旗舰，会告诉你在线强化学习到底是计划，还是借口。

## B3. "Self-Improvement" Means Two Very Different Things

**EN**
The phrase recursive self-improvement conjures a model that rewrites its own training code, and almost nobody in the industry is describing that. Live RL is the opposite shape. Nothing on the training side gets reinvented: no self-synthesised environments, no self-generated curriculum. What changes is where the data comes from. Real users hit the model in production, and their behaviour — the retries, the acceptances, the abandonments — becomes the reward signal. That is why the honest comparison is test-time training, not science fiction. The pipeline stays conventional, and the thing being removed is a dependency on people: specifically the small population of engineers who know how to run a training run well. Read this way, Google's choice stops being mysterious. A company that is losing that population but owns the largest compute budget on earth should want exactly this trade, because it converts a talent problem into a budget problem. The risk is equally clear, and the video does not hide it: a model learning from live traffic is a model learning to please live traffic, and every online reward signal is an invitation to be gamed.

> **中**
> "递归自我改进"这个词让人联想到模型改写自己的训练代码，而行业里几乎没有人在描述那种东西。在线强化学习的形状恰好相反。训练侧什么都没被重新发明：没有自合成的环境，没有自造的课程。变的是数据的来源。真实用户在生产环境里使用模型，他们的行为 —— 重试、采纳、放弃 —— 变成了奖励信号。所以诚实的类比是测试时训练，而不是科幻。流程仍然是传统的，被去掉的是对"人"的依赖，具体说就是那一小撮懂得怎么把一次训练跑好的工程师。这样读下来，谷歌的选择就不神秘了。一家正在流失这类人群、却拥有地球上最厚算力预算的公司，理应想要这笔交换，因为它把一个"人才问题"换成了一个"预算问题"。风险同样清楚，视频也没遮掩：一个向实时流量学习的模型，学的是讨好实时流量，而任何在线奖励信号都是一份"来骗我"的邀请函。

## B4. Zhipu's Bet Is on the Factory, Not the Model

**EN**
Set the three pillars side by side and a single thesis appears. Zhipu is not betting that its next model is architecturally cleverer; it is betting that the factory around the model can be made to run without outside inputs. Data is the first door to close, and the method is deliberately unromantic: self-play, rule checks, execution checks, a model acting as judge, and humans spot-checking the tail. The phrase worth keeping is self-certification — a filter plus a judge, extracting stable results from generations that are individually unreliable, and letting several instances collaborate to a quality none of them reaches alone. The second door is the environment. If agents can collect real tasks, write their own verifiers and decide for themselves whether a task converged, then tasks stop being commissioned one at a time and start being mass-produced. That is the difference between a dataset and a factory. The third door is the strangest and the most ambitious: the model already writes code, so aim it at the operators, kernels, schedulers, caches and compilers it depends on. Close all three and you have not raised a benchmark number; you have changed what one unit of compute buys. That is why the stated goal is phrased as price-performance rather than capability — and why roughly fifteen percent of the raise going to business development is a footnote, not the story.

> **中**
> 把三根柱子并排摆着看，会浮现出同一个论点。智谱赌的不是下一代模型在架构上更聪明，而是赌"模型周围那座工厂"可以脱离外部输入自己运转。第一个要关上的门是数据，手段刻意地不浪漫：自我对弈、规则校验、执行验证、模型当评审，人类只在尾部抽审。值得记住的说法是"自证有" —— filter 加 judge，从单条都不可靠的生成里提取出稳定的结果，再让多个实例协作达到谁都单独达不到的质量。第二道门是环境。如果智能体能采集真实任务、自己写验证器、自己判断任务是否收敛，那么任务就不再是一个个定制，而是可以被批量生产。这是"数据集"和"工厂"的区别。第三道门最奇怪也最有野心：模型既然会写代码，那就让它去优化它所依赖的算子、内核、调度、缓存和编译。三道门都关上，你没有抬高任何一个 benchmark 数字，你改变的是每一单位算力能买到什么。所以讲出来的目标才被表述成"性价比"而不是"能力" —— 也所以募资中约 15% 拿去做业务拓展只是一句脚注，不是故事本身。

## B5. The Ruler, and What It Actually Measures

**EN**
StepFun's move is worth studying precisely because it looks like the weakest of the three. It ships no model and no training method; it ships a measurement. The first sentence of the benchmark is a question most labs would rather not be asked: can a model improve the infrastructure that it itself runs on? Put next to Zhipu's third pillar it looks like a duplicate, but the task design gives it away. KFC asks for a correct, fast kernel inside fixed files — a well-specified problem, scored on whether the model improves across attempts. LHI hands over an issue or a bug report and demands the model read an unfamiliar codebase, locate the right modules, edit several files and keep testing until the thing runs end to end. E2EO is the hardest to fake: here you get the workload, the objective and the constraints, but nobody tells you which lever to pull, so the model has to find the bottleneck before it can optimise anything. Notice what all three have in common. None of them is a coding test. Every one of them is a test of whether the system learns, accumulates and improves while working on something hard. That is a definition of self-improvement you can actually score, which is more than the other two routes currently offer — and the scores, with Claude Opus 5 at 36.53, say the distance left is large.

> **中**
> 阶跃这一手值得研究，恰恰因为它看起来是三条里最弱的。它没发布模型，也没发布训练方法；它发布的是一种度量。基准的第一句话是一个多数实验室不太愿意被问的问题：模型能不能优化那个让它自己得以运行的基础设施？把它和智谱的第三根柱子放在一起看像是重复，但题目设计暴露了它不一样。KFC 要求在限定文件内写出正确且高效的算子 —— 一个定义清楚的问题，评分看的是模型能否在多次尝试中提升自己。LHI 甩给你一条 issue 或一张 bug 单，要求模型读懂一个陌生的代码库、定位正确的模块、修改多个文件，并持续测试直到端到端跑通。E2EO 最难蒙混：这里给你工作负载、目标和约束，但没人告诉你该动哪个旋钮，所以模型必须先找到瓶颈才谈得上优化。注意这三类任务的共同点。它们没有一个是"写代码考试"。它们考的全都是：这个系统在做一件难事的过程中，会不会学习、会不会积累、能不能越做越好。这是一个**真的可以打分**的自我改进定义，比另外两条路线目前能给出的都要实在 —— 而分数（Claude Opus 5 的 36.53）说明剩下的距离还很长。

## B6. The Third Route Is About the Harness, Not the Weights

**EN**
This is the part that most summaries of RSI skip, and it is the part with the clearest personal analogue. StepFun's route does not end in a bigger model. It ends in a model that has explored a system, accumulated experience, and written that experience down as skills stored in the harness — the scaffolding around the model, not the parameters inside it. Once you see the distinction you cannot unsee it, because it splits self-improvement into two very different investments. Upgrading weights is expensive, centralised, and something you wait for. Upgrading the harness is cheap, local, and something you can do this afternoon: better retrieval, better prompts, better verifiers, better memory of what failed last time. The video closes the loop neatly by pointing out that accumulated skills leave traces, and traces are training data — so the harness route and the weights route are not rivals, they are a pipeline. For anyone who is not running a frontier lab, this is the actionable half of a video otherwise full of hundred-million-dollar decisions: while the definition of RSI is still unsettled, the version of it you can actually practise is the one where you improve the scaffolding rather than waiting for the next release.

> **中**
> 这一段是多数 RSI 总结会跳过、却有着最清晰个人类比的部分。阶跃这条路终点不是一个更大的模型。它的终点是一个"探索过系统、积累了经验、并把经验写成 skill 存进 harness"的模型 —— 存在模型外围的脚手架里，而不是里面的参数里。一旦看见这个区别就再也看不见别的了，因为它把自我改进劈成了两种截然不同的投资。升级权重昂贵、中心化、而且只能等。升级 harness 便宜、本地、今天下午就能做：更好的检索、更好的提示词、更好的验证器、更好的"上次失败在哪"的记忆。视频漂亮地把环闭上了：积累下来的 skill 会留下轨迹，而轨迹就是训练数据 —— 所以 harness 路线和权重路线不是对手，它们是一条流水线。对任何不在前沿实验室里的人来说，这是这条满是上亿美元决策的视频里**唯一可执行的一半**：在 RSI 的定义还没定下来之前，你真正能实践的那一个版本，是去改进脚手架，而不是等下一次发布。

## B7. The Three-Trait Checklist Is the Real Takeaway

**EN**
People remember launch numbers and forget method sections, so it is worth pulling this one out. Φ-Bench did not stop at scores; it replayed the entire optimisation trajectory of models working on end-to-end tasks, and asked what the good ones do differently. Three traits came out. Plan before acting: strong models screen hypotheses cheaply and avoid committing to a rewrite before they have evidence. Learn from experiments: they design attempts so a failed attempt still yields information, holding variables steady and keeping noise under control. Be slow to conclude: they refuse to attribute a performance change to a cause until other explanations are excluded. Every one of those is a rule about attribution under noise, which is exactly what infrastructure optimisation is. You can hand the same three lines to a junior engineer tomorrow without mentioning AI once, and they would be better at their job for it. That is usually the tell that something in an AI paper is genuinely transferable: it still works after you delete the AI.

> **中**
> 人们记得发布会上那些数字，却忘了论文的方法章节，所以这一段值得单独拎出来。Φ-Bench 没有停在分数上：它回放了模型做端到端优化任务时的完整轨迹，问那些做得好的模型到底做对了什么。浮现出三个特质。谋定而后动：强模型会低成本地筛选假设，拿到证据之前不轻易承诺做大规模重写。实验求新知：它们把尝试设计成"即使失败也产出信息"，稳住变量、压住噪声。谨慎下结论：在排除其他解释之前，它们拒绝把性能变化归因到某个原因上。这三条都是关于"在噪声中做归因"的规则，而基础设施优化的本质正是如此。你可以把这三行直接交给一个初级工程师，全程不提 AI，他照样会因此做得更好。这通常是判断一篇 AI 论文是否真正可迁移的标志：**把 AI 删掉之后它还成立。**

## C2. The Chip Loop, and Why It Matters

The most quietly ambitious idea in this whole episode is the infrastructure one. Today the loop is: humans build hardware, humans write kernels, models run on top. Flip one arrow — let the model help design the kernels, the schedules, even the silicon — and you get a loop where the model improves the thing that makes it faster, which makes the next generation cheaper to train. The ecosystem already has partial evidence that this is not fantasy: model-assisted kernel and operator work is becoming normal, and there are public claims that recent large-model design decisions took suggestions generated by models. The reason this matters for anyone outside a frontier lab is that it turns AI from a consumer of infrastructure into a participant in building it, and every participant in that loop becomes harder to displace.

## C3. A Framework You Can Reuse

Here is the reusable part, stated once and for all. Whenever a company announces a grand technical strategy, ask three questions. What is it actually short of? What asset does it have that others cannot easily copy? And does the goal it defines line up so that the second answers the first? If all three line up, you are looking at strategy. If they don't, you are looking at a press release. This is not cynicism; aligning an ambition with your real assets is what competent strategy *is*. It also explains why every frontier lab sounds like it is saying something different about AGI: they are each standing on their own strongest leg, describing where they can actually walk. Holding this frame makes technology news much less hypnotic — read for the constraint, not for the noun.

## C4. Three Tasks, Three Different Kinds of Difficulty

People argue about whether models can "do engineering" without ever saying what the engineering is, which is why the task taxonomy is more useful than the scoreboard. Kernel function completion is the closed form: the interface and the semantics are handed to you, the files you may touch are bounded, and the only question is whether you can produce something correct and then make it fast. Long-horizon implementation is the open form: you receive what a human receives — an issue, a stack trace, a bug report — and you must reconstruct intent, locate the relevant modules across an unfamiliar codebase, edit several files, and keep testing until the feature actually runs. End-to-end optimisation removes the last piece of scaffolding: you are given the workload, the objective and the constraints, but not the path, so you have to locate the bottleneck before you can improve anything. Read in that order, the three tasks remove one crutch at a time — specification first, then orientation, then diagnosis. *Gloss — crutch (n.) something you lean on instead of doing the work; taxonomy (n.) a classification into ordered kinds.*

<details>
<summary>🔍 中文自查（读完英文再展开）</summary>

## C5. Where Self-Improvement Actually Lives

There is a habit worth breaking: reading every capability gain as a model gain. The third route in this episode is a counterexample, and a useful one, because the improvement lands in the harness — the retrieval, the prompts, the verifiers, the recorded memory of what failed last time — while the weights stay put. Two properties follow, and both favour the individual. Harness upgrades are cheap and reversible, so you can run many of them in the time it takes one model release to arrive. And they compound locally: a verifier you wrote for one task still works on the next one, whereas a benchmark score belongs to whoever trained the model. None of this makes weight upgrades irrelevant; accumulated skills leave traces, and traces are training data, so the two feed each other. But if you are deciding where to spend an afternoon, the asymmetry is obvious — one of these you can do today, the other you can only wait for. *Gloss — asymmetry (n.) a situation where two options are not equally available; compound (v.) grow by accumulating rather than by jumping.*

## 落地清单（中文速览 / 无英文）

按"先做什么最划算"排序：

1. **抄下 Φ-Bench 的三条特质当工作准则** —— 谋定而后动 / 实验求新知 / 谨慎下结论，调试、调参、复盘都能直接套；最要紧的是"提防把噪声读成信号"
2. **养成"三问"习惯** —— 看到任何公司战略稿，先填：它缺什么 / 它有什么不可复制的资产 / 这两者分别对得上吗
3. **区分"模型升级"与"Skill 升级"** —— 第三种 RSI 更像 skill 层面的进化；对个人同理，升级自己的知识库 / 提示词库 / 验证器，可能比等下一个更强的模型更快见效
4. **给 AI 工作流加一层"自检"** —— 别只让 AI 干活，让它顺手生成验证器（自己检查任务是否收敛），这是走向自我纠偏的最小一步
5. **盯住隐藏主线** —— 三家路线看似南辕北辙，目标里都藏着一句"不大幅增加推理成本的前提下加深有效计算深度"；**省钱 + 加深**，才是今年的真实主轴
6. **今天下午能做的那一半：升级 harness** —— 改检索 / 改提示词 / 加验证器 / 记录"上次失败在哪"，都是本地、便宜、可逆的；**升级权重只能等，升级脚手架今天就能做**（2026-10-14 补）
